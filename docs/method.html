The instrument · Evidence brought into light

How the monitor
defines its field of view

The program separates observation, selection, interpretation, and publication. Full text is never fetched. Every interpretive statement is constrained to the available abstract.

01

Daily pipeline

One path from metadata to a readable study

GitHub Actions starts the monitor at 07:00 JST. A 07:20 fallback runs only when the first run did not complete successfully.

  1. Observe

    Retrieve metadata and abstracts from Europe PMC, Crossref, and optionally arXiv.

  2. Normalize

    Convert source-specific records to a shared Paper structure.

  3. Unify

    Merge records that share a DOI, source identifier, or normalized title.

  4. Screen

    Require PopPK or pharmacometrics, AI or machine learning, and methodological evidence.

  5. Select

    Report new eligible papers; otherwise choose one unreported paper published since 2020.

  6. Interpret

    Generate four concise statements from the available abstract only.

  7. Publish

    Update Markdown, GitHub Issues, the JSON catalog, and GitHub Pages.

02

Relevance score

A bounded measure of scope alignment

The score ranges from 0 to 100. It ranks eligible papers by explicit alignment with this monitor; it does not assess scientific quality, validity, novelty, or clinical usefulness.

PopPK evidence0–30

20 points in the title and 10 in the abstract.

AI evidence0–30

20 points in the title and 10 in the abstract.

Method evidence0–30

20 points in the title and 10 in the abstract.

Intersection0–10

10 points when PopPK and AI are both explicit in the title.

R PK + AI + M + I

High70–100

Medium40–69

Low0–39

Repeated synonyms do not add extra points. Each component is based on presence or absence in the title and abstract.

03

Evidence boundary

Interpretation stops at the abstract

Titles support discovery and scoring, but not the four interpretive statements. The program does not download PDFs, visit publisher full text, or inspect supplementary material.

01

Prior limitation

What gap or problem does the abstract identify?

02

Contribution

What method, framework, or comparison does it describe?

03

New capability

What analysis or decision becomes possible according to the abstract?

04

Significance

Why might it matter for PopPK, pharmacometrics, or model-informed dosing?

When no abstract is available, the monitor states that evidence is unavailable instead of inferring a conclusion from the title.

04

Source policy

Primary sources, optional preprints

Source Role Failure behavior
Europe PMC Primary biomedical metadata and abstracts Displayed as a warning; required for normal operation
Crossref Publisher-registered articles and posted content Displayed as a warning; required for normal operation
arXiv Optional preprint supplement Rate-limit and timeout errors are logged internally but omitted from reports

05

Program structure

Small modules with explicit responsibilities

core.py

Data models, normalization, de-duplication, scoring, JSON I/O, and HTTP requests.

sources.py

Clients for Europe PMC, Crossref, and arXiv.

insights.py

Abstract-only interpretation with optional AI paraphrasing and a deterministic fallback.

literature_monitor.py

Source orchestration, new and archive selection, notifications, and state updates.

reporting.py

Canonical article records, Markdown reports, and GitHub Issues.

site_generator.py

Transforms the canonical catalog into data consumed by GitHub Pages.

data/articles.json

The canonical structured catalog.

state/seen.json

Seen identifiers, source health, and dates used to prevent duplicate alerts.

06

Known limits

A reading queue, not a systematic review

  • Keyword screening can produce false positives and false negatives.
  • Abstracts may omit important methods, validation details, and limitations.
  • The relevance score measures scope alignment, not study quality.
  • Source metadata may differ in dates, abstracts, and publication types.
  • The archive fallback is a ranked convenience sample.

07

Visual language

Selective light, quiet presence, and the window as boundary

The interface is an original digital design rather than a reproduction of a painting. It translates Rembrandt’s chiaroscuro, warm earth pigments, intimate frontal presence, and the framing logic of Girl at a Window into a restrained research interface. The window becomes a metaphor for the evidence boundary: only what the abstract illuminates is interpreted.

Light

Warm information surfaces emerge from deep umber rather than uniform brightness.

Frame

Rules, ledges, and dark margins create a clear field of attention.

Presence

Centered titles and measured pauses favor calm focus over visual noise.

Design reference: Dulwich Picture Gallery on Rembrandt’s Girl at a Window, described as the “Mona Lisa of London”. No museum image or artwork file is embedded in this site.
