Elimination phase and trough concentrations were quantified by UPLC-MS/MS, and a nonlinear mixed-effects model was applied to derive PK/PD parameters. Linear regression was performed to examine associations between baseline characteristics and trough concentrations. Logistic regression was used for initial feature screening, after which 13 machine learning classifiers were developed to predict AKI and mortality.
