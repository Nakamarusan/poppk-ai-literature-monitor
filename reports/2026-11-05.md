- **Abstract:** NONMEM is the gold-standard tool in pharmacometrics, yet much of its estimation engine operates out of sight. Routine outputs show what the engine reports, but not everything it computes along the way. The quantities needed to answer a specific question may exist transiently within the long-established Fortran-based engine, but for most modelers they remain hidden behind an implementation barrier. AI-assisted modeling changes that barrier. A user can pose a question in "vibe" language, work with an AI assistant to generate verbatim Fortran code that instruments the running engine and exposes otherwise unavailable quantities on demand for use as additional model diagnostics or in other ad hoc investigations.
