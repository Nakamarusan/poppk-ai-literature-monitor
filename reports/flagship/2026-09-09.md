## Abstract-only findings

- **Prior limitation:** Not stated in the available abstract.
- **Contribution:** Here, we introduce State, a machine learning model that predicts perturbation effects while accounting for cellular heterogeneity within and across experiments.
- **What becomes possible:** We further introduce Cell-Eval, a comprehensive evaluation framework that can be used to evaluate future models.
- **Significance:** While machine learning models offer potential for predicting transcriptomic effects of perturbation, they currently struggle to generalize across cellular contexts.
