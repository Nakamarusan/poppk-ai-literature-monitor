Authors: Ma D, Pang J, Gotway MB, Liang J
DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09079-8

## Abstract-only findings

- **Prior limitation:** However, existing chest radiographic deep learning models are limited in diagnostic scope, generalizability, adaptability, robustness and extensibility.
- **Contribution:** Not stated in the available abstract.
- **What becomes possible:** The development of Ark + reveals that open models trained by accruing and reusing knowledge from heterogeneous expert annotations with a multitude of public (big or small) datasets can surpass the performance of proprietary models trained on large data.
- **Significance:** We hope that our findings will inspire more researchers to share code and datasets or federate privacy-preserving data to create open foundation models with diverse, global expertise and patient populations, thus accelerating open science and democratizing AI for medicine.
