### Abstract-only interpretation

- **Prior limitation:** Abstract The human gut microbiome encodes a large spectrum of quorum sensing peptides (QSPs) playing a vital role in microbial communication and host-microbial interactions, yet discovery and functional characterization of novel QSPs are still limited.
- **Methodological contribution:** Here, we developed an integrated artificial intelligence (AI)-driven pipeline combining large-scale genome mining, machine learning-based screening, Alphafold3 structural modelling, and molecular dynamics simulations to systematically identify 1,670 novel candidate QSPs (cQSPs) from 12,488 human gut microbial genomes.
